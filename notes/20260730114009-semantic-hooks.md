# Semantic hooks

An idea I had about making a smart hook, but a dumb component.

You can make a very unopinionated component, and then make a hook that is a lot more opinionated, returning matching props for said component. That hook could be semantic, variant, or anything else, but the idea is you can pass very little in, and get a lot of config out.

This can work on a scale, meaning your hook can have whatever abstraction level you want, and/or only be required in certain scenarios. For example, having an unopinionated Pagination component, but then an opinionated hook to get you the recommended pattern for most pages.

In general I think hooks are relatively underused and can provide a lot of benefit, as it becomes another place to do abstraction. This helps fine-tune issues around [[20210908155508-flexibility-components]]
