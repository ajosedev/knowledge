# Avoiding vague words in writing

One tip for writing really clear technical documentation is to avoid words like:
- they
- us
- we
- that
- it

Be prescriptive! It not only avoids miscommunication when you might be referring to multiple 'theys', but it also makes it very clear who is responsible for certain action items and similar.

This is helpful even when writing short comments, as it avoids ambiguity.
