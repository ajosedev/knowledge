# Figma Connectors

Figma Connectors are a way of interfacing with a [[20250811104331-model-context-protocol-mcp]] server through Figma. Like most harnesses, you can use `@` to interact with it directly through chat windows, and similar.

For the case of [[20260814111009-figma-make]], this allows you to get some benefits of guidelines, instructions, and additional data from outside Figma and bring it into Figma.
