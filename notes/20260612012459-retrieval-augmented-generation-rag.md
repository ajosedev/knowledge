# Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)

RAG is an architecture that allows AI models (particularly LLMs) to deliver more relevant responses at a higher quality.

As LLMs are trained on large datasets at a single point in time, they refer to that dataset to generate outputs. However those are inherently finite, and exist at a point in time, leading to stale data and thus stale outputs.

RAG then allows AI models to access external data (both public and private), integrate it into its processing, and provide more accurate, up-to-date, and domain-specific data, without requiring re-training.
