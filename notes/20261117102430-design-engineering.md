- with AI, more designers are shifting into engineering spaces
- the advantages are obvious, get closer to the real world artifact
- it's something I've been pushing for since starting design systems - with tools like Braid's Playroom
- as expected though, it's not going swimmingly
- designers don't have the support they need
- effectively things are just getting chucked over the wall, and an engineer has to clean it up, validate it, and fix it
- engineers aren't been given good code that they just need to look over and merge, they usually have to rewrite it
- this is before you get into the cost of things like context switching
